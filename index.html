Grow With Us

Are you a local grower or producer passionate about agriculture? The Carp Farmers’ Market is currently accepting agriculture vendors only for the upcoming season.

Our market is proudly producer-based, meaning everything sold must be grown, raised, or produced by the vendor. This ensures an authentic, farm-fresh experience that our visitors have come to trust and love.

Why Join the Carp Farmers’ Market?

  • Reach thousands of dedicated market-goers each season
  • Promote your farm and products in a trusted, high-profile setting
  • Be part of a supportive vendor community
  • Grow your brand through our online promotions and events
  • Stay true to local—our market is known for its authenticity and quality

How to Apply

We are currently accepting applications from agriculture vendors only. Here’s how to apply:

  1. Review Our Vendor Guidelines
    Ensure your farm or agricultural products meet our market standards. Resale of goods is not permitted.
  2. Complete the Application Form
    Fill out the embedded vendor application form at the bottom of this page, including selecting dates and providing detailed descriptions of the products you intend to sell. Further information about licenses, permits, and certifications, if applicable, are provided within the form.
  3. Submit Your Application
    Once submitted, your application will be reviewed by the Market Board of Directors to assess suitability based on product type, availability, and space.
  4. Receive Confirmation
    If accepted, you will receive a confirmation email with further details on market dates, booth setup, and fees.
